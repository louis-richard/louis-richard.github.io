I am a plasma physicist specialising in the study of collisionless space plasmas. I completed my PhD in Space Plasma Physics at Uppsala University and the Swedish Institute of Space Physics (IRF) in 2024, before continuing at IRF as a postdoctoral researcher. In the summer of 2026, I joined the Theoretical Astrophysics and Plasma Physics Group at the Rudolf Peierls Centre for Theoretical Physics of the University of Oxford as a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow.

My research focuses on magnetic reconnection, plasma turbulence, and plasma waves, with a particular interest in the kinetic processes that govern collisionless plasmas. Through my Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellowship, I investigate the diversity and universality of kinetic equilibria in the heliospheric plasma.

To address these questions, I combine in situ spacecraft observations, theoretical modelling, and numerical simulations to characterise plasma equilibria and the interactions between charged particles and electromagnetic fields. By integrating these complementary approaches, my research aims to develop a deeper understanding of the statistical thermodynamics of collisionless, turbulent plasmas and the fundamental physical processes that shape plasma environments.
