I am Louis Richard, a postdoctoral researcher at the Swedish Institute of Space Physics. I use cutting-edge in situ spacecraft observations to study the fundamental physics of collisionless magnetic reconnection in Earth’s magnetosphere. My research explores key plasma processes, including magnetic reconnection, non-equilibrium kinetic effects, and plasma turbulence, aiming to improve our understanding of collisionless plasma physics.
